About this estimator

Designed for quick “roughly how far / how long?” checks.

DistanceEstimated from a macro maritime routing network rather than a vessel passage plan.
TimeEstimated distance ÷ selected constant speed. Weather, traffic and operational speed changes are not included.
FuelOnly shown when a daily fuel value is available in Vessel Profile.
ScopeMajor ports plus Norwegian offshore fields and fixed facilities. Small local harbours remain outside scope.
Safety boundaryNot for passage planning, navigation, collision avoidance, UKC or any safety-critical decision.
