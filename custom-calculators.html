Define your calculator

Variables and values

Use + − * / ^ and parentheses. Functions: sqrt, abs, sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, ln, log, exp, min and max. Angles are in radians. Separate function arguments with a semicolon; decimals accept a point or comma.

The formula uses the values exactly as entered. Include conversion factors in the formula; unit labels do not convert or validate dimensions.

Result

Only calculator definitions and their default values are saved. Calculations are not added to a log.
