WHO IT IS FOR

Useful across vessel and shore-based roles.

Marine Tools is intended for people who regularly need quick technical calculations, vessel values or planning estimates. It is designed as a practical aid that complements approved systems, procedures and professional judgement.

⚓

Vessel crews

Fast access to common fuel, engineering, electrical, tank and conversion tools during day-to-day work.

⚙

Fleet & technical teams

Use consistent calculation workflows when discussing vessels, consumption, machinery and technical questions ashore.

↗

Superintendents & operations

Quick voyage, fuel and weather context for early-stage planning, follow-up and operational discussions.

＋

Consultants & other maritime users

A compact browser-based toolkit for recurring calculations without building a new spreadsheet every time.
