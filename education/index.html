FOR EDUCATORS

Use it as a supplement — not a black box.

Marine Tools works best when students are asked to understand and validate the calculation, not simply copy the output.

01

Set a practical exercise

Give students realistic vessel data and ask them to calculate fuel use, endurance, tank quantities or electrical values.

02

Compare methods

Let students solve the task manually first, then use Marine Tools as a control or discussion point.

03

Discuss assumptions

Change speed, density, consumption or weather inputs and discuss how assumptions affect the result.

04

Send feedback

Students and instructors can suggest improvements and missing tools directly to the project.
