Remember: An h difference is a specific enthalpy change. Treat it as shaft work only after stating the first-law assumptions and signs for your device.

Learning points

  • Inside the dome: wet steam with vapour quality 0 ≤ x ≤ 1.
  • At x = 0: saturated liquid. At x = 1: saturated vapour.
  • At fixed pressure: h = hf + x(hg − hf), and s = sf + x(sg − sf).
  • Outside the dome: this model does not calculate superheated or subcooled states.

See IAPWS-IF97 for the authoritative industrial water/steam formulation. The educational saturation points below are an interpolated subset of publicly reported reference values, not the full formulation.
