# Psychrometric relations

The moist-air model uses liquid-water saturation coefficients and ideal-mixture relations documented by [PsychroLib](https://psychrometrics.github.io/psychrolib/_modules/psychrolib.html), with a deliberately restricted 0–50 °C teaching domain. The ASHRAE coefficients are attributed to the 2017 ASHRAE Handbook — Fundamentals. Process balances, plotting, input validation and learning activities are implemented locally in Marine Tools.
