Marine Tools app data

Draft for owner approval — local-calculator candidate 0.2.0. This document has not been approved as a public privacy policy.

Calculator inputs, vessel profiles and calculation history are stored locally in the app or browser. This candidate does not enable app analytics, advertising or crash telemetry and does not provide live AIS or weather services.

Copying, sharing, opening a website and exporting a file are actions you choose. Shared files and clipboard content are handled by your operating system, chosen destinations and recipients. Device backups may include local app data according to system settings. Android app backup is disabled in this candidate.

On mobile, an exported file is first written to the app's private temporary cache and then offered through the system share sheet. Save a copy to keep it. Temporary files may be removed by the operating system; old cache exports are cleaned on a later app start. Desktop export uses a file save dialog.

The separate Education workspace requires a connection and protected website access. Its authentication and website data handling are separate from the local calculator package. Other external websites and email applications have their own privacy practices.

Use Settings to clear supported calculator drafts, history or all local Marine Tools data. Existing exported files, recipient copies, clipboard content and device backups are not erased by clearing app storage.

Questions: contact@marinetools.app.

No passwords, account tokens or signing keys should be entered in calculator fields. Local storage is not a credential vault.
